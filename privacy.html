How We Handle Your Information

Privacy Notice

Last reviewed: [date to be completed]

The Ordo Militantium Reginae Caeli asks for personal information in only two places on this site: the contact form and the membership application. This notice sets out what is collected, why, where it goes, and how to have it removed.

✦ ✦ ✦

Who is responsible

[The party responsible for this information, and the address for privacy enquiries, to be completed before publication.]

What we collect, and why

The contact form asks for your name, email address, the nature of your enquiry, and your message. It is used to answer you, and nothing else.

The membership application asks for your first and last name, email address, date of birth, country of residence, diocese, the apostolate that interests you, your reasons for applying, and your confirmation that you are a practising Catholic, that you are eighteen or older, and that what you have written is true. It is used to consider your application and to contact you about it.

We ask for a date of birth only to confirm the age requirement, and for a diocese only so that an application can be placed in the right province. We do not ask for, and you should not send, financial information or identity documents.

That some of this is sensitive

An application records that you are a practising Catholic. Information about religious belief is treated as a special category under data protection law in the United Kingdom, the European Union, and elsewhere. We collect it because membership of a Catholic order requires it, and we collect it only with your consent, given when you submit the form. You may withdraw that consent at any time by asking us to delete your application.

Where it goes

Submissions are delivered by Web3Forms, a third-party service that relays the contents of the form to the Order by email. Web3Forms handles the message in transit; the Order holds it thereafter. Nothing you submit is stored on this website, which has no database.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose servers keep ordinary access logs, including IP addresses. Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which means Google receives a request from your browser when you view a page.

[Retention period to be completed: how long applications and enquiries are kept, and what happens to an unsuccessful application.]

What you may ask of us

You may ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to us through the contact page and say what you are asking for. We will not require you to explain why.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising. It stores one thing in your browser: a note of which announcement you have dismissed, so the same notice is not shown to you twice. That note never leaves your device and is not read by the Order.

Those under eighteen

Membership is open only to those aged eighteen or over, and the application will not accept a date of birth below that age. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the head of this page changes with it.
